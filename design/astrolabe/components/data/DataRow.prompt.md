One stated fact, with the dotted leader that makes the app read like an almanac index.

```jsx
<DataRow mark="☉" label="Sun" value="16° 42′ Virgo" />
<DataRow label="Houses" value="Whole sign" tone="soft" />
<DataRow label="Engine" value="Swiss Ephemeris 2.10" small />
```

- Use it in runs of three or more; a single DataRow looks like a mistake.
- `small` is the provenance block at the foot of an instrument.
