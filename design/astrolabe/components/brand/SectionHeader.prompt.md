Opens a section on a scrolling screen — Home's "Her latest readings", Sky's "What is coming".

```jsx
<SectionHeader eyebrow="From Shruti" title="Latest readings" action="See all" onAction={go} />
<SectionHeader title="What is coming" rule mark="♄" />
```

- Use the rule sparingly: at most twice on a screen, or the gold stops being special.
- Titles are sentence case. Never Title Case, never an all-caps heading.
