Rate limits and caching

updated 2026-08-25·by Shruti edit this page →

Sixty requests a minute per address, and a day of cache on anything older than yesterday. Neither number is interesting; both are stated because an instrument that fails quietly under load is worse than one that says no.

The limits

Exceed them and you get 429 with a Retry-After in seconds — a refusal with a number in it, like every other refusal in the service.

HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests
Retry-After: 24
X-Reckoned-By: theourgia-ephemeris 0.9.4

Nothing here needs a table of contents, which is the point of the state: a page with fewer than three headings gets small print in the rail instead, and the body column stays exactly where it is on every other page.
