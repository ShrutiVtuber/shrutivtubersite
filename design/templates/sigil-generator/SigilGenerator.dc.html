The reduction consumed the sentence

Every letter was a vowel or a repeat, so the letter set came out empty. There is no figure to draw and the instrument will not invent strokes to fill the plate.

01Keep the vowels this time — a short statement may need them. Keep vowels
02Say it at greater length. Sigils are usually better from a full sentence than a word.
03Draw it by hand. The tool is a convenience, never a requirement.

How it is made

The sentence is stripped to letters, repeats and vowels struck out, and what remains is laid over itself until the shape stops reading as writing. The step where it stops being a sentence is the point of the exercise, and it is the step this page shows you rather than hides.
