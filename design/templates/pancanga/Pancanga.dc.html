○ Cannot be reckoned

The day has no beginning here

The vāra starts at sunrise, and at 78.22°N the Sun does not rise today. Tithi, nakṣatra, yoga and karaṇa can still be given — they depend on the Moon and Sun in longitude, not on the horizon — but the day they belong to is undefined.

01Show the four limbs that do not need a sunrise, and mark the vāra undefined. Do that
02Reckon the day from midnight instead, labelled as a substitution rather than a tradition. Switch reckoning
03Keep the latitude. The pañcāṅga was not built for the pole, and saying so is more useful than a number.

How it is reckoned

A tithi is the Moon gaining twelve degrees on the Sun, so it is not a day and does not keep a day's length. A nakṣatra is the Moon crossing one of twenty-seven equal sidereal quarters. Both end when the arithmetic says they end — mid-morning, mid-meal, mid-sentence — which is why the ending time matters more than the name.
