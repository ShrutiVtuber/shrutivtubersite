The Sun does not rise here today

At 78.22°N on this date the Sun stays below the horizon, so there is no sunrise to divide. The planetary hours are undefined here — not zero, not midnight-to-midnight. The instrument says so rather than inventing an answer.

What practitioners do instead, and what this page can offer:

01Equal hours from local midnight — clock hours, honestly labelled as such. Switch to equal hours
02Reckon from the nearest date the Sun clears the horizon — 18 Feb 2027 here. Use that date
03Keep the latitude and change nothing — the answer stays undefined, which is itself the answer.

How it is reckoned

Sunrise to sunset is divided into twelve equal hours; sunset to the next sunrise into twelve more, so their length breathes with the season. The first hour belongs to the day's ruler — the Moon, this Monday — and the rest follow the Chaldean order: ♄ ♃ ♂ ☉ ♀ ☿ ☾.
