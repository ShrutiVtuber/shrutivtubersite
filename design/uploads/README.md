## Why Clouds.png matters more than it looks

Shruti is a multi-tradition initiate: Hellenic theurgy under Hekate, Śākta
Tantra (Mahāvidyā), and Thelema. Those three traditions converge on **the
twilight junctures** — *sandhyā* literally means the junctures, dawn and dusk,
when tarpaṇam and mantra practice are performed; Greek practice anchors to dawn,
dusk and the lunar stations; her phone app is built around moonrise,
culmination, moonset and nadir.

`Clouds.png` is, independently, a dusk sky.

So **dawn and dusk are the subject, not the decoration.** Light and dark themes
are the same sky at its two junctures rather than one inverted into the other.
This is the strongest thesis available and it is true of the practice, the
software, and the art she already owns.

## What does not exist yet

icon set. Art arrives slowly and much of it is physically in Athens. **Every
component needs a designed art-absent state that looks deliberate**, with the
art-present state as the enhancement — not the other way round.
