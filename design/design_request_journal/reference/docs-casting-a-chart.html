Casting a chart

By Shruti · Updated August 25, 2026

The ephemeris is a small HTTP service. Every instrument on this site is a page over it, and it answers the same questions for anyone who asks.

What you need

A moment and a place. The moment is a date and a time; the place is a latitude and a longitude. Nothing else is required, and nothing else is stored.

```

GET /chart?date=1990-05-04&time=14:20&lat=37.9838&lon=23.7275

```

What comes back

Both traditions, side by side and unranked. The tropical zodiac and the sidereal one are not two attempts at the same answer — they are two different questions, and the response says so by giving you both rather than choosing.

FieldMeaning
tropicalSign and degree measured from the equinox
siderealRāśi and nakṣatra measured from a fixed star point
speedPerDayDegrees per day — negative means retrograde
housesWhole-sign and the chosen quadrant system
svgThe figure, drawn server-side

Birth time you do not have

If you do not know the time, say so rather than guessing noon. The ascendant moves roughly a degree every four minutes, so a guessed time gives you houses and angles that are confidently wrong.

Pass time_unknown=true and the response omits the angles and the houses, and returns the planetary positions alone. Those are accurate to within the day. A chart that admits what it cannot know is worth more than one that fills the gap.

Rate and licence

There is no key and no rate limit worth mentioning. The service is AGPL-3.0 and runs Swiss Ephemeris; if you use it over a network, the licence asks that your users can reach the source, and the response headers carry the link to the exact running commit.
