Your reading
♍ Virgo

A month for finishing, which is not the same as a month for starting.

Mercury, your ruler, spends the first fortnight in your own sign and the second in Libra, which is the difference between thinking about your own work and thinking about someone else’s opinion of it. Do the private part first.

The stationary retrograde of Saturn on the 9th sits in your seventh, so an agreement you have been carrying alone stops being carryable alone. Say the true thing about it early in the month, while there is still room to alter terms rather than only to leave.

The letter
