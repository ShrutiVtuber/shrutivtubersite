## Fixed while assessing

Not cosmetic; each was live and silent.

1. ⚠ **The Discord bot linked to a page that did not exist.** Every
   announcement it has ever posted carried `{site}/practice/{id}`, and there
   was no such route. Written.
2. ⚠ **`label()` threw on a `covers` it could not parse**, taking down any
   page that rendered that period with a 500.
3. ⚠ **The shared secret was never wired into compose**, so the Discord bridge
   could not run in either direction — the site logged one line per submission
   and carried on.
4. ⚠ **Twelve backend guards had stopped guarding**, failing on a missing file
   rather than on the thing they check. They had been red long enough to read
   as "those always fail".
5. ⚠ **The practice and device models were absent from the metadata**, so an
   autogenerated migration would have written a DROP for both.
6. ⚠ **The app's ephemeris had no Jupiter and no Saturn** — the table stopped
   after Mars, so they were missing from the ephemeris rather than from the
   screen.
7. ⚠ **A route-order shadow** (`/bridge/vote` under `/{work_id}/vote`) caught
   by the guard before it shipped. Fourth time in this codebase; first time
   something other than a person found it.

## Two deliberate refusals, not failures

`account_test` and `practice_test` refuse to run unless pointed at a local
stack: they make accounts and post readings, and against the real site that is
junk in her practice room. They fail loudly with the reason. Run them with

    adb reverse tcp:8200 tcp:8200
    flutter test integration_test/practice_test.dart \
      --dart-define=SHRUTI_SITE=http://127.0.0.1:8200
