# Handoff: `/compatible` — chart comparison page

## Overview

`/compatible` is the front door of shrutivtuber.com for people who have never heard of Shruti. It
exists to run one loop: two VTubers compare their birth charts → they get an image worth posting →
they post it and tag each other → their audiences click through → some of them make one → repeat.

Its job is to make a stranger want to do the thing within about eight seconds, and to make the
result worth posting — while remaining recognisably the same site as `/today`. Instruments are
exact, verdicts are hand-written, nothing claims predictive validity.

This bundle covers three deliverables:

1. **The page** — `/compatible`, top to bottom, every state.
2. **Three share card designs** — the 1200×630 image generated server-side by Pillow.
3. **Two promotional images** — for Shruti's own launch post (not generated by the app; static art).

Plus two follow-up surfaces designed alongside the page but out of its formal scope: the invite
landing (`/chart/invite/…`) and the comparison result (`/chart/compare/…`).

---

## About the design files

The files in this bundle are **design references created in HTML** — prototypes showing intended
look and behaviour, not production code to copy. The target is Astro 6 + Tailwind, zero-JS by
default, per the site's existing stack. The task is to **recreate these designs in
`frontend/site/src/pages/compatible.astro`** using the site's existing components and token
stylesheets, not to ship the prototype HTML.

Two consequences worth stating up front:

- The prototypes are **React under the hood** (a streaming component runtime). Ignore the runtime.
  Read them for markup structure, token usage, copy, and state behaviour.
- The prototypes use **inline styles exclusively** because of how the prototype runtime paints.
  In the real page, use Tailwind classes mapped through `tailwind.preset.js`, or plain CSS — every
  value in the prototypes is already a `var(--token)` reference, so the mapping is mechanical.

## Fidelity

**High-fidelity.** Colours, typography, spacing, radii, shadows, motion durations, copy and all
state behaviour are final. Every colour is a semantic token — there are **zero hardcoded colours on
the page**. Recreate pixel-for-pixel using the existing token stylesheets.

One deliberate exception: the **share card art** (§ *Share card* below) is drawn server-side by
Pillow and takes literal hex. Those hex values are final and are database rows, not code.

## Share card — the real deliverable

Generated server-side at **1200×630** by `backend/shruti/core/sharecard.py`. Renderer capabilities:
flat fills, pasted images, circle masks, lines, rectangles, TrueType text. **No gradients, no blend
modes, no blur, no shadow, no rounded rectangles.** Nothing specified here needs a compositor.

### Layout (positions are code; these are the recommended values)

Margin 72px on all sides. Three bands, distributed top / middle / bottom:

**Top band**
- Two 92px circle-masked avatars at `x = 72` and `x = 144` (20px overlap). Art-absent avatar: flat
  disc filled with the *names* colour at 12% alpha, a 1px ring in the *names* colour at ~50% alpha,
  and the initial centred in EB Garamond 40px in the *names* colour.
- Names at `x = 262`, baseline-aligned with the disc centres: Commissioner 27px weight 500 in
  *names*; the word “and” in *marker*.
- A 1px full-width-of-content rule in the *note* colour at ~38% alpha, ~26px below.

**Middle band**
- **Verdict** — EB Garamond 88px, weight 500, `line-height .98`, `letter-spacing -.015em`, in
  *verdict*, left-aligned, wrapping at 20 characters, two lines maximum.
- 20px below: one line containing the **count** (JetBrains Mono 27px, tabular, in *count*) then the
  **top marker** (JetBrains Mono 22px, tabular, in *marker*), separated by 16px.

**Bottom band**
- A 1px rule in *note* at ~38% alpha.
- 18px below: the **disclaimer** left (Commissioner 19px in *note*) and the **site name** right
  (JetBrains Mono 20px in *note*).

### The four layout changes from the current implementation

1. **The verdict is the card.** 88px, left-aligned, starting at the optical centre. Centred between
   two avatars it reads as a caption. It is the only line that must survive downscaling to a phone
   thumbnail.
2. **Avatars smaller, paired, top-left.** Overlapped they read as a pair; on either edge they read
   as a versus graphic. Smaller also stops the art-absent state looking like a failure — and since
   avatars are uploaded by each chart's own owner, half-empty pairs will be common.
3. **Count and top marker on one line, immediately under the verdict.** They are the evidence for
   the verdict. This is also what stops the card reading as a horoscope: a number and a named aspect
   are right there.
4. **Disclaimer on the bottom rule, opposite the site name.** 19px minimum, never at reduced opacity.
   Pairing it with the URL on one ruled footer makes it structural furniture rather than small print
   — the only way a disclaimer survives a future redesign.

### Two asks for the renderer

- **Alpha on flat fills** — the scrim, the hairlines (~38%) and the art-absent avatar discs (~12%).
  An RGBA layer plus one `Image.alpha_composite` covers all three. Still flat fills; no compositor.
- **A wrap-and-shrink rule for the verdict** — wrap at 20 characters; if it still exceeds two lines,
  step the size down 88 → 76 → 68px. “Written in the same sky” is the longest of the five current
  bands and fits at 88px, so the ladder only fires on a band added later.

## Interactions & behaviour

**Server-rendered, works with scripting off.** Everything below must degrade to something usable.

| Behaviour | Detail | No-JS fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Cycling verdict word in the hero | every 2600ms: opacity → 0, wait 210ms, swap word, opacity → 1. `transition: opacity 200ms var(--ease-out)` | first band renders statically; the line still reads correctly |
| “I don't know my birth time” | removes the time input from the DOM and swaps the left column's copy for the branch block | a plain checkbox; the server honours it on submit and the result names the two absent testimonies |
| Form submit | client-side validation first (below), then one request | native form POST; server returns the page with the error block or the done state |
| Copy buttons | `navigator.clipboard.writeText`, label → “Copied” for 1800ms | the value is visible as selectable text in every case |
| Card hover | `transform: rotate(0deg)` + `--shadow-2` over `var(--dur-2) var(--ease-out)` | static, unrotated is fine |
| Share links | plain `<a href>` intent URLs | identical — they are just links |

**Validation rules** (client and server, same messages):

- no birth date → “A birth date is needed — without it there is no chart to cast.”
- no birth place → “A birth place is needed. Start typing a city and pick one from the list.”
- consent unticked (and not signed in) → “The consent below has to be ticked before a chart can be
  kept.” — plus `error="This one is required."` on the `ConsentCheckbox` itself.

The error block sits **above** the form: `role="alert"`, 1px `--live` with `border-left-width: 3px`,
`background: var(--live-wash)`, radius 10, `padding: 15px 17px`. Title in `--live` weight 600 —
“One thing is missing” / “Two things are missing” (count-aware). Then a `<ul>` of messages in `--ink`.
Failed fields get `aria-invalid="true"`.

**Motion.** Fades and small translates only, `--ease-out`, `--dur-1`/`--dur-2`; the sky cross-fades
over `--dur-3` on theme change. No bounces, no parallax, no scale on press — press is
`translateY(1px)`. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses transitions to 1ms via
`tokens/effects.css`, **and the hero's cycling word must not start its interval at all** — check
`matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` before scheduling.

**No engagement patterns.** No countdown, no popup, no fake scarcity, no “1,247 people compared
today” unless it is a real count you are willing to show the query for.

**Responsive.** Tiers <640 / 640–1024 / 1024+ / 1440+. Every grid uses
`minmax(min(100%, Npx), 1fr)` — the `min(100%, …)` is what guarantees no sideways scroll at 320px.
All tap targets ≥44px. AA contrast in both themes.

**Themes.** Dawn is canonical; Dusk follows the OS via `prefers-color-scheme`. **No switch on the
page.** Because everything is a semantic token, Dusk is free — a single hardcoded colour breaks it.
`.sky-stars` appears only at dusk.

|---|---|---|---|
| `form` | `'idle' \| 'done'` | successful submit | swaps the form card for the done state in place |
| `noTime` | boolean | the “I don't know” checkbox | removes the time input; swaps the left column copy; changes the done-state body line |
| `consent` | boolean | the consent checkbox | gates submit when not signed in |
| `errors` | list of messages | failed submit | renders the alert block; sets `aria-invalid` |
| `signedIn` | boolean | session | replaces the consent checkbox with a one-line account notice |
| `designCount` | 1 \| 3 \| 5 | `card_design` rows | showcase grid; count label |
| `adminBlocks` | 0…n | page blocks | the admin section, or its total absence |

**Signed-in must not look broken.** The consent checkbox is replaced — not just removed — by a
quiet `--surface-inset` plate, 1px `--line`, radius 10: eyebrow “Signed in”, then “Your account
already carries this consent, given 14 March 2026.” (tabular mono date), then a “Review or withdraw”
link. The form is genuinely one field shorter and the card keeps its rhythm because that row still
exists.

**Done-state body copy** varies with `noTime`:
- known → “{name}'s chart is cast and kept behind the link below. Nothing else was stored.”
- unknown → “{name}'s chart is cast without a time, so two testimonies are set aside and named on
  the result. It is kept behind the link below.”

`{name}` falls back to “Someone”.

**Unknown birth time** — the designed branch, left column:
- Eyebrow with the `☾` glyph in `--rose`: “Without a time”.
- Display line at `--text-prose`: “Then it says so, instead of guessing at it.”
- Explanation: no Ascendant and no houses, so two of the seven testimonies cannot be weighed; the
  count reads **out of five**, and the two left out are named on the result rather than quietly
  dropped.
- A two-row mono table on `--surface-card` plates, 1px gaps over a `--line` background:
  “Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Saturn by sign → weighed” (`--accent`) and
  “Ascendant, house rulers → cannot be weighed” (`--rose`).
- Closing `--ink-faint` line: find the time later and the chart takes it and recounts; nothing has
  to be cast again.

The site's whole character is that it says what it cannot compute rather than guessing. This branch
is that rule applied to the most common gap in the input.

`--page-pad` `clamp(16px,4vw,40px)`.

**Radii.** `--radius-sm` 4px controls · `--radius-md` 10px cards · `--radius-lg` 16px sky panels ·
`--radius-full` pills. Share card images use a bare `2px` — they are flat art, not UI cards.

**Shadows.** `--shadow-1` resting cards · `--shadow-2` hover · `--shadow-3` floating layers only
(used here for the hero card, which is deliberately reading as an object lying on the sky).
Never decorative glows.

**Motion.** `--dur-1` 120ms · `--dur-2` 240ms · `--dur-3` 600ms ·
`--ease-out` `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)` · `--ease-in-out` `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.25,1)`.

**Focus.** 2px `--focus-ring` outline, offset 2, always visible — from `tokens/effects.css`.

**Glyphs.** Astronomical glyphs are **type, never emoji**: `☾` `○` `◐` `●`. Always set on a span
carrying `.t-glyph` (`font-variant-emoji: text`) and pair the character with U+FE0E in the source,
or browsers substitute a colour-emoji font that ignores `color`. No emoji anywhere on this site.
Moon-phase marks carry meaning: `○` public · `◐` named, not detailed · `●` under seal. The `●` on
the keep-this-link block is deliberate — that link is the one secret on the page.
